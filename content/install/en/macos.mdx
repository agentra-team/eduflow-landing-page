<Steps>

<Step n={1} id="chon-tep" title="Pick the right file for your chip">

Open the Apple menu, choose **About This Mac** and read the **Chip** line.

| The Chip line says | File to download |
|---|---|
| Apple M1, M2, M3 or newer | `EduFlow-<version>-arm64.dmg` (Apple Silicon) |
| Intel | `EduFlow-<version>.dmg` (Intel) |

EduFlow runs on macOS 12 or later.

</Step>

<Step n={2} id="cai-dat" title="Install into Applications">

Double-click the **.dmg** file you just downloaded, then drag the **EduFlow** icon into the **Applications** folder. After that you can eject the installer disk.

</Step>

<Step n={3} id="mo-lan-dau" title="First launch">

Open EduFlow from **Applications** or Launchpad. The installer is signed with Developer ID and notarized by Apple, so macOS asks for confirmation only once because the app was downloaded from the Internet. Click **Open**.

</Step>

<Step n={4} id="kich-hoat" title="Activate or start a trial">

On the Welcome screen:

1. If you have a license key from your school: enter the key, which starts with `EDU-` followed by groups of 4 characters, and click **Kích hoạt** (Activate).
2. If you do not have a license key yet: click **Dùng thử** (Trial), then fill in your full name, email, phone number and organization. When Agentra has trial auto-approval turned on, the computer receives a trial key right away; otherwise the request waits for approval and the app activates itself once the request is approved.

A trial key works on 1 computer, with a trial budget and an expiry date.

</Step>

<Step n={5} id="ho-so" title="Choose a work profile">

Choose the role you use most: K-12 teacher, lecturer, head of department, Quality Assurance Office, Program Council or school leadership. A profile only arranges the interface, locks no feature, and can be changed in **Cài đặt** (Settings).

</Step>

<Step n={6} id="cap-nhat" title="Get new versions">

When a new version is available, the app shows a notification. Click **Tải về** (Download), keep working while it downloads, then click **Khởi động lại để cài** (Restart to install). Your projects and data stay as they are.

</Step>

<Step n={7} id="go-cai" title="Uninstall">

Quit EduFlow, then drag **EduFlow** from **Applications** to the Trash. Project data lives in the app's data folder; if you need to keep it, create a backup in **Cài đặt** (Settings), under **Lưu trữ và sao lưu** (Storage and backup), before uninstalling.

</Step>

</Steps>
