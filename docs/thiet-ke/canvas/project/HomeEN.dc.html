How EduFlow works

Five steps, with a person deciding at the last one

Every step leaves a trail: who did it, when, and against which standard.

  1. 01

    Set up

    Choose the standards framework, subject, course or degree program records. You can start with a single sentence to the assistant.

  2. 02

    AI fills in structured content

    The app builds a fixed structure; AI only fills in the content under a strict data contract.

  3. 03

    Quality gates check

    Each gate prints how many units it checked. A gate that checked 0 units counts as an error and never reports a pass.

  4. 04

    You review and sign

    AI content carries the Suggestion label until it is approved. Signing locks the version, and its hash is notarized.

  5. 05

    Export and share

    Export PDF, DOCX, PPTX or a signed package for the head of subject group, the head of department or the Quality Assurance Office.

Transparency and safety

The machine suggests, people decide

Your data stays on your computer

Projects, artifacts and chat history stay on your computer. The server only relays content to the model and records token counts; it never stores content.

Three tiers of conclusions, never mixed

The deterministic tier states facts with their basis. The comparative tier says “not found yet” with the number checked. The suggestion tier always needs human review.

Checking 0 units does not mean clean

Every quality gate prints how many units it checked; a gate that checked nothing counts as an error.

Signatures and hash notarization

Signed versions are locked against edits. The server notarizes the hash without seeing the content, and package recipients can verify it.

Works offline

Quality gates, recompiling, signing and exporting files keep working offline. Only the AI parts need a connection.

Transparent costs

Every AI task shows an estimated cost before it runs and the actual cost after it runs.

Questions and answers

Frequently asked questions

What is a license key and where do I get one?

Each school, faculty or individual receives one license key for an agreed number of computers. Send a request right in the app or contact Agentra to get a key.

How do I try it?

Download and open EduFlow, click Try it on the first screen and fill in the form. A trial key works on 1 computer, with a trial budget and an expiry date.

Do I need an Internet connection?

Yes, to activate, to run AI tasks and to ask the assistant. Quality gates, recompiling, signing and exporting files keep working offline.

Where is my data stored, and what does the assistant send?

Projects and artifacts stay on your computer. The assistant only sends a structured summary of the open screen, never the original files. The server does not store content.

How am I charged?

By the AI tokens used, with a monthly budget per key. What runs on your computer is free. Details are on the Pricing page; schools can contact Agentra for a quote.

How many computers can one key be used on?

As many as the number of devices on your organization's key. When you change computers, an Agentra administrator revokes the old one to free a slot for the new one.

Can a school turn off AI for a project?

Yes. Turn on the AI off switch in the project details and the app sends no AI calls for that project, including the assistant; everything that runs on your computer keeps working as usual.

How do I update to a new version?

The app tells you when a new version is available. Click Download, then Restart to install; your projects and data stay as they are.
